# Career identity and motivation

This file is read on every scoring call alongside your CV(s). It is what lets
the tool judge *interest fit*, not just *skill fit*, so vague input gets vague
scoring. Write it like you are explaining yourself to a thoughtful mentor, not
filling in a form. Replace every section below; the prompts are scaffolding.

## Who I am professionally

Two or three paragraphs. What you do, for how long, in which industries, and
what you are genuinely good at. Say what you are known for, not what a job
title says.

## My academic background

Degrees, the field, and anything from it you still use. One paragraph is
plenty.

## What I'm moving toward

The kind of work, team and company you want next. Concrete beats aspirational:
name the problems you want to work on, the size of company, the culture, the
technologies if they matter to you.

## What I'm moving away from

The roles, industries or setups you no longer want, and why. This is what
stops the tool surfacing a role you are qualified for but would never take.

## Non-negotiables that aren't captured in constraints.yaml

Anything a location, workload or office-days filter cannot express: travel
share, on-call, people management or not, language of the workplace, and so
on. One line each.
