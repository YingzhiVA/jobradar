# Interview stories (STAR)

Put your prepared interview stories in this directory as Markdown — either
one file per story (the **filename** becomes the story's label in
`interview_prep.md`, so name it after the story, e.g. `turned-around-launch.md`)
or one file holding all of them with a `#` heading per story.

These are first-person accounts of your key accomplishments in
Situation/Task/Action/Result form. They should expand on roles already in
your CVs — extra depth, context, and results — not introduce employers,
titles, or dates the CVs don't have, since documents generated from them
must stay consistent with the CV you submit.

The stories feed three things:

1. **Skill-fit scoring** — extra evidence of demonstrated skills, so the
   scorer can credit experience a terse CV bullet undersells.
2. **Cover letters** (and CV bullet phrasing) — the concrete
   situation-and-result details that make a claim credible.
3. **Interview prep** — `python -m jobradar.apply --prep <folder>` maps a
   posting's likely interview questions onto your stories and flags the
   requirements no story covers yet.

This directory is optional: everything works without it, you just lose the
three uses above.
