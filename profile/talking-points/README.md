# Talking points

Standard answers to questions that recur in every interview and would
otherwise get re-improvised each time — differently worded, and worse under
pressure. "Why are you leaving?", "why this industry?", "what are you looking
for?", and the like.

Distinct from the neighbouring directories on purpose:

- `profile/stories/` — STAR accomplishments. Loaded into scoring, cover
  letters, and prep sheets.
- `profile/evidence/` — published technical write-ups. Loaded into the apply
  pipeline.
- `profile/talking-points/` — **this directory, read by no pipeline.** These
  are positioning answers about your situation, not evidence of what you have
  done. A "why I'm leaving" framing has no business leaking into a cover
  letter, so nothing loads it. It is here to rehearse from before an interview.

This directory is optional. If you use it, one file per question works well,
and carrying the answer in each language you interview in (the interview is
usually held in the posting's language) saves translating under pressure.
